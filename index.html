Inside the hunt

Features

Built for the way car people already behave — at shows, in parking lots, on the road.

Collect

Your Registry

Every verified spot files itself under the right model. The collection is the score — no currency, no pay-to-win.

Grade

Full Dossier

Five graded angles on one car for a complete record. Rare metal is worth walking around for.

Chase

Bounties & Most Wanted

Owners put their car up as a bounty. Hunters go find it. Everyone wins something.

Gather

Scheduled hunts

Post a hunt, everyone RSVPs, and the lobby opens when it starts. Shows, meets, cars and coffee.

Compete

Crews & the board

Ranks, titles, streaks and a live leaderboard across the Registry, legends and streak tables.

Share

Reels & share cards

Every spot exports as a vertical reel or card, ready to post without leaving the app.

House rules

Fair play

A spotting game only works if the spots are real. These are non-negotiable.

Live captures
The camera is the only way in. Nothing from the camera roll, ever.
Real metal
Screens, prints and posters get caught and don't count.
No duplicates
The same shot can't be filed twice, by you or anyone else.
Owner's call
Bounties are owner-declared. We don't publish where anyone's car lives.
