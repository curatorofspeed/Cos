Tyre Hunt

A camera-first car spotting game. Photograph a car on the street or at a show, the app identifies it, and it goes into your Registry. The bigger your collection, the higher you rank.

  • Live photos only. No screenshots, no old camera-roll shots.
  • Full dossiers, bounties, crews and scheduled hunts.
  • Launched at Monterey Car Week 2026.

F1 Card Index

The price guide for Formula 1 trading cards. Real sold prices from the major auction houses, organized by driver, so you know what a card actually went for.

  • Top sales and price history for every driver.
  • Checklists for current drivers, legends and F2/F3 prospects.
  • Email alerts when a driver's cards come up for sale.

How we build

Made at the curb

We test at car shows, cars and coffee, and auction previews. If it doesn't work one-handed in a parking lot, it isn't done.

Real data only

Real photos, real sold prices, real race results. We'd rather show less than show something made up.

Small and fast

Lean products that load quickly and stay out of the way. We ship, watch how people use it, and tune from there.

Built by a small team of gearheads who'd rather be at the track.
